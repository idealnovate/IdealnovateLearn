Why Attend

What This Circle Diagnoses — And Fixes

DCC exists so no member figures it out alone. Here's what walking into the Circle actually gets you.

Tailored Challenge Discovery

Instead of generic advice, you get a real "clinic" evaluation of your specific tech, career, or business bottleneck. Facilitators and peers help isolate the root cause — a weak portfolio, wrong pricing, broken execution — and build a clear, step-by-step fix.

Radical Peer Accountability

Passive learning leads to burnout. DCC pairs members into high-accountability pods that meet bi-weekly to track goals, review progress, and make sure plans actually get executed — not left unfinished.

Practical Skill Monetization

DCC focuses heavily on financial return: direct guidance, playbooks, and feedback on turning raw digital skills into income — client acquisition, pitching, service packaging, pricing, freelancing and business models.

High-Value Network & Collaboration

Get direct access to Mr. Favour Francis, industry mentors, and a community of ambitious peers. Physical quarterly meetups build strong local bonds — opening doors to co-founding, project partnerships, referrals, and shared talent.

Access to Exclusive Growth Resources

Members get curated tools, pitch deck templates, portfolio frameworks, gig opportunities, and workshop recordings — built to accelerate professional growth and business scaling.

The Lineup

Six Sessions. Zero Filler.

Here's what's actually happening on the day — every segment built to move you from stuck to shipped.

01

Tech Wahala & Open Heart Roundtable

Participants share their current roadblocks and bottlenecks that have kept them from scaling and monetizing their skills or business.

02

Pitch My Broken Project

Members pitch their tech skill monetization strategy using different monetization approaches. The funniest and most convincing pitch wins a prize!

03

Smart, Skilled, but Broke: The Way Out

A conversation confronting a frustrating reality facing thousands of African professionals: high intellect, technical expertise, and multiple certifications — yet constant low income, delayed payments, and financial stagnation.

04

Network, Fun and Games

Designed to break the ice, spark spontaneous networking, and turn traditional corporate networking on its head through lighthearted competition and collaborative play.

05

Portfolio Review Corner

An interactive, peer-led diagnostic session where members bring their real-world work out of isolation to get actionable, real-time feedback.

06

DCC Opportunity Vault

An exclusive marketplace segment connecting community members directly to real-world financial opportunities, client leads, and strategic partnerships.

Six sessions. One room. Miss it and you'll be hearing about it secondhand.
